About

I'm a 3rd year Ph.D. candidate researching 5G network and protocol security. My work focuses on identifying and analyzing vulnerabilities in 5G protocol implementations, with an emphasis on vulnerability detection and responsible disclosure.

I have discovered and responsibly disclosed numerous CVEs in widely deployed 5G core implementations — including a critical authentication bypass — with research papers currently under submission. Beyond academia, I bring industry experience from Apple and Agoda, where I worked on large-scale software engineering and security challenges. I'm driven by a commitment to bridging security research and real-world engineering to make networked systems more secure.

Research Interests: 5G Security, Network Protocol Analysis, Vulnerability Detection, Fuzzing, Software Engineering
